  en:
    title: "Małopolska-Style Minced Pork Schnitzels"
    description: "Traditional Małopolska-style minced pork patties with soaked bread. The TM6 chops and sautés onion and garlic, then mixes the pork; the patties are fried in a regular frying pan. Suggested settings: check at the first cook."
    ingredients:
      - name: "Minced pork"
        note: "ideally pork shoulder, not extremely lean mince"
      - name: "Onion"
      - name: "Stale white bread roll"
      - name: "Milk"
        note: "for soaking the bread"
      - name: "Egg"
      - name: "Garlic"
        note: "peeled clove; the TM6 will chop it"
      - name: "Salt"
      - name: "Black pepper"
      - name: "Dried marjoram"
      - name: "Breadcrumbs"
        note: "for coating the patties"
      - name: "Rapeseed oil"
        note: "8 g for sautéing onion and garlic in the TM6, remaining 22 g for pan-frying"
    steps:
      - text: "Tear the stale bread roll into pieces, pour the milk over it and leave for a few minutes until softened. Gently squeeze out excess liquid."
      - text: "TM6: peel the onion and garlic, cut the onion into quarters, and place both in the mixing bowl to chop."
      - text: "Scrape the chopped onion and garlic down to the bottom of the bowl and add the portion of oil reserved for the TM6 (8 g). Sauté with the measuring cup removed, placing the simmering basket over the lid to prevent splashing. The onion should soften without burning."
      - text: "Transfer the sautéed onion and garlic to a shallow plate to cool. Do not add them to the meat and egg while hot. There is no need to wash the TM6 bowl."
      - text: "TM6: add the minced pork, squeezed bread, cooled onion and garlic, egg, salt, pepper and marjoram. Mix in reverse just until combined, avoiding a smooth paste. Scrape down and finish mixing briefly by hand if needed."
      - text: "Divide the mixture into six equal portions. With damp hands, shape slightly flattened oval patties about 2 cm thick."
      - text: "Lightly coat each patty in breadcrumbs and shake off any excess. Do not press the coating down too hard."
      - text: "Heat the remaining oil (22 g) in a frying pan over medium heat. Fry the patties for roughly 4–5 minutes per side until golden brown. Keep the heat moderate so the coating does not brown before the centre is cooked."
      - text: "Check the centre of the thickest patty with a meat thermometer. If it has not reached 75°C, reduce the heat, cover briefly and continue frying while checking again. Do not rely on the colour of the coating alone."
      - text: "Serve immediately, ideally with boiled potatoes and beetroot or Polish cucumber and sour cream salad."
