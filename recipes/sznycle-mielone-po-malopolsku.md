      - text: "Chop the onion very finely. Heat a small amount of the measured oil in a frying pan, soften the onion over medium-low heat without browning, then let it cool completely."
      - text: "TM6: place the minced pork, squeezed bread, cooled onion, egg, garlic, salt, pepper and marjoram in the bowl. Mix in reverse to combine, but avoid turning the meat into a smooth paste. Scrape down with a spatula; if not evenly mixed, finish combining briefly by hand."
      - text: "Divide the mixture into six equal portions. With damp hands, shape slightly flattened oval patties about 2 cm thick."
      - text: "Lightly coat each patty in breadcrumbs and shake off any excess. Do not press the coating down too hard."
      - text: "Heat the remaining oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Fry the patties for roughly 4–5 minutes per side until golden brown. Keep the heat moderate so the coating does not brown before the centre is cooked."
      - text: "Check the centre of the thickest patty with a meat thermometer. If it has not reached 75°C, reduce the heat, cover briefly and continue frying while checking again. Do not rely on the colour of the coating alone."
      - text: "Serve immediately, ideally with boiled potatoes and beetroot or Polish cucumber and sour cream salad."
